The remote preview is a proportional pilot design choice based on the inspected package and current authentication boundary, not proof of usable latency, accessibility or installed container capacity. The future live gate must measure it with a representative multi-file 011 scene. The owner selects the feedback category explicitly; `mixed` follows factual requalification as Conversation 23 requires. No additional classifier or unresolved routing decision is assumed.
